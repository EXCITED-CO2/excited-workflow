## 0.0.1 (2025-03-26)

First released version of the EXCITED-workflow. The workflow can generate models and predictions for the region Transcom 2 with Ameriflux data, as a proof of concept.

The models are trained on Fluxnet and CarbonTracker data.

The input datasets are;
- ERA5
- ERA5-land
- ESA CCI Land cover
- SPEI
- MODIS
- Terrestial live biomass

The [documentation pages](https://excited-workflow.readthedocs.io/) have instructions for new users on how to set up the workflow on their system, and how to download the data.
